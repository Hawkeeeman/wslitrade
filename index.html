03 — Security

Your keys. Your network.
Your rules.

WSLI never exposes trading infrastructure to the public internet. Tailscale provides encrypted peer-to-peer connectivity — only devices on your tailnet can reach the agent.

Zero Public Exposure

Agent APIs and dashboards served via tailscale serve — no open ports, no reverse proxy headaches.

WireGuard Encryption

All traffic between nodes encrypted end-to-end through Tailscale's mesh.

ACL Policies

Granular tailnet ACLs control which devices can talk to the trading agent.

Self-Hosted

Runs on hardware you own. No SaaS middleman holding your strategies or credentials.
