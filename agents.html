Research priorities

Weekdays · 4:30 p.m. Eastern

  • Stop width versus noise. The current 0.1 × daily ATR stop also appears in the published ORB study; it is not automatically a coding error. Test alternatives on held-out observations with the same dollar risk. One quick stop-out is not enough evidence to change the strategy.
  • Feed and fill mismatch. Signals use one exchange (IEX); paper fills use NBBO. Check whether a breakout exists on comparable market data. Alpaca’s simulation rules · IEX versus SIP
  • Measure execution, not just win rate. Track stop slippage, holding time, rejected signals and net results after realistic costs. Paper simulation omits important live-trading frictions.
  • Do not inherit a paper’s claimed returns. The study tests a broad universe and both long and short trades. WSLI currently uses a much smaller verified universe, long-only trades and one position at a time. This is a different experiment; its performance must be measured independently.

Research proposes tests. It cannot place orders or automatically rewrite the strategy. Useful new findings are reported; repetitive findings stay quiet.
