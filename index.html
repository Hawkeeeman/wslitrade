About us

We train the machine.

WSLI is a small team. We design, train, and run an autonomous trading bot for US equities — a system that watches the tape and acts with the standards we would demand of ourselves.

The bot is not a rented model. We train it ourselves: which names to follow, how to read a setup, when to size in, and when to stand down. The goal is simple. Stay in the market. Take only the trades the machine was built to take.

How it works

From scan to fill.

The WSLI bot is trained by us to read stocks and make disciplined decisions. Once the session opens, it watches, judges, and executes without a hand on the book.

  1. 01

    Taught by us

    We train WSLI on the names we follow — how a setup looks, how much risk to take, and what to ignore. Its decisions reflect our process.

  2. 02

    Reads the session

    Through the open it scans its universe and evaluates each stock against the rules it was taught to respect.

  3. 03

    Decides with discipline

    It sizes risk, passes on noise, and only acts when conviction and the book line up. Accuracy over activity.

  4. 04

    Takes the trade

    When the standard is met, the machine executes. Positions, the live feed, and every fill land on the Agents floor.
