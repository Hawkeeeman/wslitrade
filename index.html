About us

We test the process.

WSLI is a small team researching systematic trading in US equities. We combine fixed rules, recorded market data, and bounded AI reviews.

Gem reviews saved evidence using an existing AI model. Saving context is not training model weights. Deterministic code—not an unchecked AI answer—controls the small paper-trading experiment. Execution testing is not proof of alpha.

01

Built in-house

We build fixed research rules, observation tools and a bounded paper executor. The new account is separate from earlier experiments.

02

Grounded in evidence

Saved snapshots and citation checks keep reviews tied to observed numbers. They do not guarantee correct reasoning.

03

Risk first

Cash-sized limits, a daily entry cap and broker protective orders constrain the pilot. Missing or stale data are refused; gaps and outages can still exceed nominal risk.

How it works

From scan to evidence.

The agents separate data collection, AI oversight and deterministic execution. A running monitor, a candidate and a filled order are different things.

  1. 01

    Rules set in advance

    The strategy and screening rules are saved before the session, not rewritten after seeing the outcome.

  2. 02

    Reads the session

    The collector records opening bars and sampled IEX quotes. IEX is one exchange, not a consolidated view of the market.

  3. 03

    Reviews the evidence

    Short AI reviews cite saved observations. A validator checks those citations; no review authorizes an order.

  4. 04

    Records real paper outcomes

    The execution journal and broker responses distinguish attempts, partial fills, stops and closed positions. Small paper results are not live returns or proof of long-term profitability.
