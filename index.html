About us

We test the process.

WSLI is a small team researching systematic trading in US equities. We combine fixed rules, recorded market data, and bounded AI reviews.

Gem reviews saved evidence using an existing AI model. Saving context is not the same as training model weights. The current experiment observes opening-range setups; execution and performance validation remain separate work.

01

Built in-house

We build the research rules and observation tools. A trading execution engine is not part of the current pilot.

02

Grounded in evidence

Saved snapshots and citation checks keep reviews tied to observed numbers. They do not guarantee correct reasoning.

03

Risk first

The pilot has no order-submission method. Missing or stale data are rejected, and observations are not reported as fills.

How it works

From scan to evidence.

Today’s pilot separates data collection from AI review. Gateway health, heartbeat attempts, observations, and broker activity are different signals.

  1. 01

    Rules set in advance

    The strategy and screening rules are saved before the session, not rewritten after seeing the outcome.

  2. 02

    Reads the session

    The collector records opening bars and sampled IEX quotes. IEX is one exchange, not a consolidated view of the market.

  3. 03

    Reviews the evidence

    Short AI reviews cite saved observations. A validator checks those citations; no review authorizes an order.

  4. 04

    Tests before execution

    Historical evaluation, realistic costs, and tested execution safeguards must come before a new paper-trading trial. Existing account holdings are not pilot results.
